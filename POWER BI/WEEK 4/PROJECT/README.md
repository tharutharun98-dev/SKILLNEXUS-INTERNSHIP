## Assignment
1. Create a dashboard with 4 visuals:
   - Sales by Region (Map)
   - Category-wise Profit (Bar Chart)
   - Monthly Revenue Trend (Line Chart)
   - Top 5 Customers (Table)
2. Add filters for **Year**, **Segment**, **Region**.
3. Add company logo and theme colors.
4. Publish to Power BI Service and share the link.
